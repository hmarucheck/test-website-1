Details

How the marketplace runs

Search and ranking

Search results are ordered by relevance to the query, seller rating and delivery record. Sponsored listings exist, but they are labelled and shown separately from organic results.

  • Sponsored placements are labelled
  • Filters persist between sessions
  • Stock counts come from seller inventory

Payments

Sellers are paid within two business days of delivery. Buyer refunds are issued when the carrier scans a return.

Packaging

Paper-based packaging is the default across the seller network, with plastic-free materials available at cost.

Support

Support is handled by staff rather than automated replies. Median first response is currently 11 minutes.

Account data

Veyloro does not sell browsing data. Account data can be exported or deleted from your settings page, as set out in the Terms of Service.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Veyloro?

Veyloro is an online marketplace for independent sellers. Shops are reviewed before they open, and there are currently around 40,000 of them across eight categories.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive within two to four business days. Tracking is available from your order page as soon as the seller hands the parcel to the carrier.

What does buyer protection cover?

Buyer protection applies to every order. If an item does not arrive, arrives damaged, or does not match its description, you can open a claim from your order page and Veyloro reviews it directly.

How do returns work?

Most items can be returned free within 30 days. You print a prepaid label from your order page, and the refund is issued once the carrier scans the parcel.

How much does it cost to sell?

Opening a shop is free and there are no listing fees or monthly plans. Veyloro charges a flat 5% commission when an item sells, and payouts reach your bank within two business days.

Is there an app?

There are Veyloro apps for iOS and Android with saved shops, order tracking and restock alerts. This site can also be installed as a web app from your browser menu.

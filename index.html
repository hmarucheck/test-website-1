Sellers are paid within two business days. Buyers are refunded the moment a return is scanned. Nobody waits on the other.

Questions

Everything people ask about Veyloro

What is Veyloro?

Veyloro is a curated online marketplace focused on you. Every shop is reviewed by a real person before it opens, so you browse 40,000+ independent sellers instead of an endless wall of near-identical listings.

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in two to four business days. Every parcel ships carbon-neutral, and tracking updates land in your inbox and in the Veyloro app the moment your seller hands the order over.

Is shopping on Veyloro protected?

Yes. Veyloro Buyer Protection covers every order. If an item never arrives, arrives damaged, or isn't what was described, you're refunded in full — no arguing with the seller required.

How do returns work?

Returns are free for 30 days on almost everything. Print a prepaid label from your order page, drop the parcel off, and the refund is issued as soon as the carrier scans it.

How much does it cost to sell?

Opening a shop is free — no listing fees, no monthly subscription. Veyloro takes a flat 5% when an item sells, and payouts reach your bank within two business days.

Does Veyloro have an app?

Yes. The Veyloro app for iOS and Android carries your saved shops, order tracking and restock alerts — and this site installs as a web app straight from your browser menu.
